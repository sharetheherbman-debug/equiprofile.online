# UK Supplier Research Notes

**Research date:** 2026-08-21

## WeatherBeeta EU wholesale portal

- Official wholesale-application page: <https://b2b.weatherbeetaeu.com/company/account/create>
- The portal explicitly offers an application for a **wholesale account** and an account is submitted for review.
- The form requests business type, trading/delivery address, VAT and EORI details where applicable, retail-premises information, commercial-premises status, business activity, stock investment, existing suppliers, estimated initial order, and estimated annual purchase value.
- The visible intended product categories include rugs and sheets, lifestyle and competition footwear, bridlewear, apparel, stable and grooming, and headcollars and leads.
- No public API, CSV/XML feed, dropship model, MOQ, account cost, image-reuse license, retailer delivery SLA, or returns policy was established from the reviewed application page. These must be treated as **not publicly verified / commercial agreement required**.

## Jenkinsons Equestrian Wholesalers

- Official site: <https://jenkinsonsequestrian.co.uk/>
- The site identifies itself as supporting the equestrian retail trade and provides a **Trade Log In**.
- Its visible catalogue navigation covers Animal Health, For the Horse, For the Rider, Gifts Toys and Cards, Horse Clothing, Shop Display, and Stable Field & Yard.
- The official page links delivery details, terms and conditions, account management, deliveries, orders, and payments, but the reviewed public landing page did not establish public API/feed availability, dropship availability, MOQ, trade-account costs, image-use licensing, retailer delivery SLA, or returns terms.
- Those unverified fields must remain **not publicly verified / commercial agreement required**, with any source imagery marked **IMAGE_RIGHTS_REVIEW_REQUIRED** until express rights are documented.

## CAMEO trade account

- Official trade-account page: <https://cameoequine.com/apps/wpdapp>
- CAMEO explicitly invites wholesale customers to apply for discounted prices, a tailored wholesale shopping experience, and additional benefits.
- The public application asks for a business address, company, website, phone, tax ID, two supplier references, closest competitor, time in business, and legal business form.
- The visible range includes ladies’ riderwear, junior riderwear, equine products, bridlework, leatherwork, and custom bridle products. The official navigation also advertises retail partnerships on Shopify Collective.
- The reviewed page does not establish feed/API access, automated stock or price update mechanism, dropship availability, MOQ, account cost, permitted image use, delivery SLA, or trade returns model. These remain **not publicly verified / commercial agreement required**.

## Equetech stockist programme

- Official stockist page: <https://www.equetech.com/pages/become-a-stockist>
- Equetech publicly describes a reseller/stockist programme for equestrian clothing and accessories, including competition wear, jackets, breeches, and base layers.
- The published stockist benefits explicitly include a full stock-supported range, a trade stock-level website and ordering system, small initial opening order, dropship orders for out-of-stock items, no order minimums, and fully optimised website images.
- The page does not publish an API specification, CSV/XML feed details, account cost, stock-refresh cadence, price-refresh cadence, delivery or returns SLA, VAT handling, or explicit image license scope. Those details require a commercial agreement. Despite the mention of optimised website images, use must remain **IMAGE_RIGHTS_REVIEW_REQUIRED** until the agreement grants the intended publication rights.
