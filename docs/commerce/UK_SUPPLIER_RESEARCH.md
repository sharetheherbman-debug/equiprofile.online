## Evidence-handling policy

The Store keeps supplier factual data, generated marketing copy, supplier source payloads, approvals, and audit actions as separate records. It will not hotlink supplier images; it will not reproduce supplier descriptions without permission; and it will not activate a supplier from this document. Any future connector must use credentials supplied under an authorised agreement and record source timestamps, stock freshness, and import provenance.
